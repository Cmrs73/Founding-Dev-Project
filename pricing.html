Pricing FAQ

Questions about pricing.

Need a custom quote? Book a call.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. A free account gives you 30 days of building credit and up to 3 active apps, so you can try it on real work before you pay.

How do I pick a paid plan?

Sign up free, then choose Unlock or Unleash. You can upgrade or switch plans at any time.

What is building credit?

It's what the AI uses while it builds and changes your apps. Paid plans add credit every month, and unused credit rolls over. You can top up any time.

What does it cost to publish an app?

Landing pages publish free. Each published full stack app is $9 per month if it can sleep when no one is using it, or $18 per month to stay always on. Business includes always-on hosting.

Do you charge per seat?

No. Plans bill the team, not the seat. Add as many people as you need.

Do I own what I build?

Yes. On a paid plan you can download your code at any time and put your apps on your own domain.
