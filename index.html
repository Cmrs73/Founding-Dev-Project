FAQ

Questions we always get.

Can't find your answer? Ask us directly.

Do I really own it?

Yes. You own the code, the data, and the infrastructure. Nothing shuts off if you stop paying us. On a paid plan, you can download your code at any time and use your own domain.

Who maintains it, and what if it breaks?

We do. We run the servers, handle scaling, keep everything updated, and fix things if they break.

Is it secure and compliant?

Yes. Your software comes with bank-level encryption, daily backups, and full audit trails. We support SOC 2 and HIPAA where your business needs them.

Isn't custom or AI-built software risky?

Apps start from the work of experienced engineers. You can make changes in the editor with expert support, or have our engineers make them. Your data is never used to train AI.

How does moving my data over work?

We handle the full migration from your old tools, with no data loss. Your records, customers and history all come over.

What does it cost?

Ready-made apps start on a flat monthly plan. Custom builds have a one-time build fee, then an optional flat maintenance fee. There are no per-seat charges. Most teams pay up to 70% less than their old SaaS. See pricing.

What kind of support do you offer?

Every plan includes dedicated support. Migrations and customizations are handled by our team, or by AI agents around the clock.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No long-term contracts. Cancel anytime and export all your code and data.
