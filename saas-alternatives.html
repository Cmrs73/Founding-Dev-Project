FAQ

Thinking about switching?

What does it mean to own my software?

You own the code, the data and the infrastructure. You can download your code, use your own domain, and nothing shuts off if you stop paying us.

Is it really cheaper than my current tools?

Usually. There are no per-seat fees, so the cost doesn't climb as you grow. Most teams pay up to 70% less than their old SaaS.

Why are per-seat fees a problem?

Every new hire makes your bill bigger, so you end up rationing access to the tools your team needs. With one price, everyone can use it.

Should I build, buy or own?

Buying SaaS is quick, but you rent forever. Building with a dev shop gives you ownership, but it's slow and every change costs extra. Owning with Founding Dev gives you a working app today that you can change yourself.

Do I need a developer?

No. Start from a template and customize it in plain English. Our engineers are there when you want them.

What happens to my data if I stop paying?

You keep it. You keep your code and your data, and you can export everything.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Apps come with bank-level encryption, audit trails and single sign-on from day one. Your data is never used to train AI.
